FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for the Digital Nomad residency?

Eligibility depends on your professional situation, work relationship, income and supporting documentation. We can review your circumstances before you begin.

Can my family be included in my application?

In many residence procedures eligible family members may be included or apply as accompanying family members, depending on the route.

Can you help with residency renewal?

Yes. We can assist with document review and practical preparation for eligible renewals.

What is Homologación?

Homologación is an administrative procedure related to recognition of certain foreign academic qualifications in Spain.

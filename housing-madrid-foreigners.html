Finding Housing in Madrid as a Foreigner

Updated August 2026

Finding a home is not only about the listing price. For newcomers, the practical challenge is matching neighbourhood, transport, school/work needs, rental documentation and contract timing.

What to organise first

Before viewing properties, prepare a realistic housing file: identification, evidence of income or financial capacity and any other documents an owner or agency may reasonably request. Never send sensitive information indiscriminately; verify who you are dealing with.

Think about the whole case

Spain’s Urban Leases Act provides the legal framework for urban rentals, but individual contracts and current rules matter. Read the contract carefully, understand what is included, and clarify deposits, duration, utilities and notice provisions before signing.

Use current official information

For Madrid specifically, compare total commuting time and daily services, not just distance on a map. A slightly farther neighbourhood with strong transport can work better than a central address with poor fit.
